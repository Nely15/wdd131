Dulces Detalles Gift Shop Site Plan

Site Name

This name was chosen because it reflects the thoughtful and meaningful gifts offered by hte online gift shop. "Dulces Detalles" translates to "Sweet Details", emphasizing special gifts for memorable occasions.

Site Purpose

Dulces Detalles is an online gift shop where customers can browse and purchase gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, graduations, and other special occasions. THe website will showcase products, featured gift collections, shopping cart functionality, and a contact form.

Scenarios

  • What gifts are available for a birthday within my budget?
  • How can I contact the store if I have questions about a product?
  • What gift items are currently featured for special occasions?

Color Schema

  • Dark Pink (#bf0f5b) - headings, buttons, accents
  • Cream White (#f5e9db) - page background
  • Dark Gray (#333333) - body text and footer

Typography

  • Roboto Slab - used for headings and branding
  • Montserrat - used for body text, navigation, and buttons
